Stride ("we") helps men connect around real-world activities. This policy
explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

## What we collect
- **Account:** phone number and/or Apple/Google sign-in identifier, used to
  authenticate you. Phone verification gates hosting and joining.
- **Profile:** display name, optional photo, optional bio, and a coarse area
  label. You provide these.
- **Location:** when you allow it, your device location is used to find nearby
  activities. We store activity venue locations precisely but **only ever show
  other users an approximate area + distance** — never your exact location.
- **Activity & social:** games you host/join, chat messages, connections,
  invites, event RSVPs, blocks and reports you file.
- **Strava (optional):** if you connect Strava, we store **summary stats only**
  (distance, time, elevation) for recent activities to show on your profile.
  We do **not** store GPS routes or start points.
- **Apple Health (optional, iOS):** if you grant access, recent workouts are read
  **on your device** to display on your profile. This data is **not** sent to or
  stored on our servers.
- **Notifications:** an Expo push token, to send game/chat notifications.

## Why we use it (legal bases)
- To provide the service and the features you ask for (contract).
- Safety: blocking, reporting, and men-only/verification checks (legitimate
  interest, and your protection).
- With your **consent** for optional integrations (location, Strava, Apple
  Health, push notifications) — which you can withdraw at any time.

## Who we share it with
- **Supabase** (hosting, database, auth, storage) — our processor.
- **Expo** — push notification delivery.
- **Strava** — only if you connect it, to fetch your activities via OAuth.
- We do **not** sell your data or use health/fitness data for advertising.

## Retention
We keep your data while your account is active. Delete your account (Profile →
Privacy & data → Delete account) and we erase your profile, games, messages,
connections and linked Strava data, and revoke Strava access. Some safety
records may be retained where required.

## Your rights
- **Access / portability:** export your data as JSON (Profile → Privacy & data).
- **Erasure:** delete your account at any time, in-app.
- **Withdraw consent:** disconnect Strava / Apple Health, or revoke location and
  notification permissions in your device settings.
- Contact us at the email in `SUPPORT_EMAIL` for any request.

## Children
Stride is for adults (18+) only.

## Changes
We'll update this page and the "last updated" date when this policy changes.
